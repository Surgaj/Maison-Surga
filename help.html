A few things to know.

When can I shop?

Maison Surga is currently testing its new storefront against the existing Wix commerce system. The final launch catalogue is still in preparation. A current store product may be used during testing, but final pricing, product details and supplier information will be reviewed before public launch.

Delivery & returns

The staging storefront can open the existing Wix checkout so we can verify the purchase flow. Final delivery destinations, costs, timings and returns terms will be reviewed before the new storefront becomes the public Maison Surga site.

About the images

The photography on this preview is AI-created editorial imagery. It illustrates our visual direction and does not confirm final products, features or packaging.

Privacy & cookies

The staging storefront uses Wix Headless authentication and eCommerce services. A Wix visitor or member session is stored in browser storage so the shopping bag and sign-in state can persist across pages. No advertising pixel is enabled in this storefront code. A complete privacy and cookies notice will be reviewed before public launch.
