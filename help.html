A few things to know.

Shopping

You can explore the current Maison Surga edits now. Product availability and current price are shown on each product page. Delivery, taxes and the final total are confirmed again at secure Wix checkout before payment.

Delivery & returns

Available delivery options, destination eligibility and any delivery charge are shown at secure checkout for the order being placed. Please keep your order confirmation. Return eligibility and instructions depend on the specific order and item, so do not send an item back without confirming the applicable return instructions first.

About the images

Some editorial brand imagery is AI-created and illustrates Maison Surga’s visual direction. Product pages load the current product media from the live catalogue.

Privacy & cookies

The storefront uses necessary browser storage for features such as your cart, sign-in session and favourites. Secure sign-in and checkout are handled by Wix and may use necessary cookies for those flows. No advertising analytics are enabled in this storefront code.
